International License (CC BY 4.0)**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

本倉庫的數據（`data/`、`sources/registry.csv`、`methodology/` 內所有內容）以
**創用 CC 姓名標示 4.0 國際授權（CC BY 4.0）** 發佈。

## How to attribute / 如何標示出處

> HK Open Carbon Platform contributors, *香港開源碳核算平台 HK Open Carbon Platform* (version X.Y.Z), CC BY 4.0.

Please also cite the original source of each factor (the `source` field), as many
values come from government or company publications.

請同時引用每個系數的原始來源（`source` 欄位）。

## Not covered / 不在授權範圍內

- Files in `sources/archive/` are copies of third-party documents kept for
  verification. Their copyright stays with the original publishers.
- 商業數據庫（如 ecoinvent）的數據不會收錄於本倉庫。
